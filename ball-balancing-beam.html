Mechanical design / feedback control

One axis.
A balancing act.

A completed personal project with Neil: a ball-balancing beam that connects printed mechanical parts with sensing and servo control.

CAD & 3D PRINTINGARDUINO UNOSG90 SERVOULTRASONIC SENSING

01 / The build

Keep the ball near a target.

An HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor measures the ball’s position along the beam. An Arduino UNO uses the position feedback to command an SG90 servo, which changes the beam’s tilt through a linkage.

The center pivot supports the beam while the servo controls its angle. The project brought mechanical geometry, sensor alignment and control behavior into one physical assembly.

02 / My role

The mechanism behind the movement.

My work focused on the beam, pivot, base, servo mount, linkage and sensor mounting. I worked with Neil on assembly, calibration and testing, connecting the mechanical design with the sensing and control system.

Neil focused on the electronics and control side. Working together gave us a practical way to connect mechanical design decisions with the behavior of the finished system.

03 / System

The feedback loop

Sense → compare → tilt → repeat.

Ball position → ultrasonic sensor → Arduino control → servo and linkage → beam tilt → ball movement.

The build uses one servo for one axis, with 3D-printed parts forming the mechanical assembly.

04 / Outcome

A completed mechatronics project.

Completed the physical build, assembly, calibration and testing. The project combines CAD, 3D printing, mechanism design and feedback control.

This overview describes the build and our roles. Quantified balancing accuracy, settling time and repeatability results are not published here.
